Choosing a hookah flavour is easier when you start with the profile you already enjoy. Fresh mint-forward blends feel crisp, while fruit blends offer a sweeter, lighter direction.

At AL Cafe & Lounge, our team can help you narrow the menu by intensity and flavour family. For a first visit, choose one clear profile rather than combining too many notes at once.

Ask about premium selections and base add-ons when you arrive. They are an easy way to tailor the experience to your group.
